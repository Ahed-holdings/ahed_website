**Page 6 --- Managed Services**

Suggested headline: Reliable Business Support, Built Around Your Needs.

This page needs special attention because managed services can mean very
different things to different clients.

I would define your offering as the ongoing management or coordination
of agreed business-support processes, using defined deliverables,
reporting and service standards.

Potential offerings, depending on the delivery capability you establish:

-   Management reporting and MIS preparation

-   Finance operations coordination

-   Accounting process support

-   HR administration and payroll coordination

-   SOP and compliance-calendar administration

-   CRM and sales-reporting support

-   Vendor and procurement coordination

-   Management review and action tracking

-   Business process monitoring

-   Outsourced functional coordination

For every offering, explain:

1.  What the service covers.

2.  What the client must provide.

3.  What you or your delivery team will perform.

4.  How frequently it will be delivered.

5.  What reports or outputs the client receives.

6.  What remains the responsibility of the client or its statutory
    professionals.

Desired outcome: Dependable support functions without requiring the
client to build every capability internally.

Only list services you can genuinely deliver, either directly or through
a reliable professional network.

**10. Final recommendation: your core message**

The website should have one consistent message across every page:

You help businesses make the transition from ambition to structured,
sustainable performance.

Your six service areas explain what you do. Your methodology explains
how you work. Your background, examples and insights explain why clients
should trust you.

That combination is stronger than simply listing six departments.

Before developing the final copy, I would settle on the consultancy\'s
brand name, whether the website represents you personally or a
registered firm, and whether managed services will be delivered by you,
employees or partner professionals. Those decisions will determine the
right tone, credibility claims and scope of the website.
