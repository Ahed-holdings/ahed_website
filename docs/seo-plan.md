This plan is written for someone learning SEO. Each phase says **what** we do, **why** it works, **who** does it,

---

## The basics in 2 minutes

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **SEO** | Search Engine Optimisation: making a site easy for Google to understand and trust, so it shows higher in free (non-ad) results. |
| **Keyword** | The words people type, e.g. "business consultant Kochi". Each page should clearly answer a few related keywords. |
| **Ranking / position** | Where the page appears for a keyword. Position 1–3 gets most clicks; page 2 (position 11+) gets almost none. |
| **Impression / click / CTR** | Impression = your result was shown. Click = someone clicked. CTR = clicks ÷ impressions. |
| **Title / meta description** | The blue headline and grey text in a Google result. They decide whether people click. |
| **Local SEO** | Ranking for searches with a place ("in Kochi", "near me"). Driven by the website **and** the Google Business Profile. |
| **Local pack** | The map with 3 businesses shown above normal results for local searches. Comes from Google Business Profiles. |
| **Backlink** | Another website linking to ahed.in. Each good one is like a recommendation. |
| **Citation / NAP** | A listing of the business (Justdial, Sulekha…). NAP = Name, Address, Phone: must be identical everywhere. |
| **Structured data** | Hidden, machine-readable business details on the site that Google reads (name, services, areas served). |
| **Indexing** | Google storing a page so it can show it. A page that isn't indexed can't rank. |

## Phase 1 · Website: local keywords (developer)

Why: Google ranks a page for "business consultant Kochi" when the page clearly talks about that service **in that
place**. Right now the site barely mentions the cities in its titles and text.

**1a. Titles, descriptions and business details**
- [x] Meta titles and descriptions optimised (titles ≤ 60, descriptions 120–160, no city names in titles; cities in
      descriptions)
- [x] Richer business details for Google: "ProfessionalService" with services and areas served (hidden data)
- [x] Breadcrumb trail for every page and Service details on the service pages (hidden data)
- [x] Photo descriptions (alt text) for the six service photos (hidden)
- [x] Expertise keywords (knowsAbout) in the business details (hidden)

**1b. One page per city** (strongest on-site step for local search)
- [ ] /business-consultant-kochi
- [ ] /business-consultant-kottayam
- [ ] /business-consultant-ahmedabad

Each page is **genuinely different** (local business types, how Ahed works there, the services most relevant,
a contact button). Copy-paste pages with the city name swapped are ignored by Google.

**1c. Links between pages**
- [ ] City names in the footer linking to the city pages
- [ ] Service pages link to the city pages and vice versa

Done when: all pages live, re-indexed in Search Console (URL inspection → Request indexing).

## Phase 2 · Keyword research and measurement (you)

Why: we should write for what people actually search, and measure what works instead of guessing.

**2a. Keyword research (free)**
- [ ] Google Ads account → Tools → **Keyword Planner** → "Discover new keywords"
- [ ] Try: business consultant, management consultant, business advisory, SME consultant, HR consultant,
      ERP consultant, business plan consultant, family business consultant, each + Kochi / Kottayam /
      Ahmedabad / Kerala / Gujarat
- [ ] Note the monthly searches for each in a sheet: keyword · searches/month · which page targets it
- [ ] Share the sheet: pages and articles get written around the best ones

**2b. Measurement routine (every Monday, 10 minutes)**
- [ ] Search Console → **Performance**: tick *Average position*; check **Queries** (what people searched) and
      **Pages** (which page they landed on). Filter queries by "kochi", "kottayam", "ahmedabad"
- [ ] Search Console → **Pages**: anything new under "Not indexed"? (Old 404s from the domain's previous owner are fine)
- [ ] Netlify → **Forms → contact**: number of enquiries this week
- [ ] Optional: free **Ahrefs Webmaster Tools** (verify via Search Console) for rankings and backlinks

**2c. Analytics (decide later)**
- [ ] Choose Google Analytics or a privacy-friendly alternative, to see which page/search brings each lead.
      Needs a small site change (and the security policy allowing it).

## Phase 6 · Results and what to expect

| When | Realistic result |
|---|---|
| Now | Brand searches ("Ahed") show the site; first impressions and clicks |
| 1–3 months | City pages and service pages start ranking for less competitive searches (e.g. "business consultant Kottayam") |
| 3–6 months | Stronger positions as reviews, listings and articles build up; first steady organic enquiries |
| 6+ months | Competitive searches (e.g. "business consultant Kochi") within reach, if the routine continues |

SEO compounds slowly. Nothing reliable is instant; anyone promising "page 1 in a week" is selling spam.

**Monthly KPIs to report to the client:** impressions, clicks, average position for the city keywords, number of
enquiries (form + calls + WhatsApp), number of reviews, number of listings/backlinks.
